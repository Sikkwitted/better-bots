            Your bots work on their own

            Give a bot a task and it keeps working until the task is done. It uses the tools on the computer Better Bots is installed on without asking you first. If that’s this computer, that means your files, your apps and your accounts.

            Bots can read and change files, run commands, use the internet, and send, post, delete, pay and change passwords with the accounts you connect.

            Run Better Bots only on a computer you’re comfortable letting bots use, and keep backups. To make bots ask first, open App settings → Permissions and choose Ask.
